We bring clarity to ad spend.

7Captur is a small, focused team. The aim is a system precise enough to trust and conservative enough that you never have to trust it blindly.

7Captur was built after watching the same pattern repeat across e-commerce accounts: a platform-reported ROAS that looked healthy, and a margin that did not move the way it should have.

The gap was never a mystery once cost of goods, returns and processing fees were laid out next to the ad numbers. Almost nobody had the time to do that reconciliation every week. Founders were not afraid of one bad number. They were tired of not knowing, week to week, whether the spend was working.

The product does that reconciliation automatically. The approval model exists so the automation never has to be trusted blindly. Every proposed change is small, capped, reversible and shown with any relevant data lag.
