Process & Mechanism

From account access to an executed decision, in four steps.

Onboarding is a single session. After that, the pipeline runs on its own, every week, until you tell it otherwise.

The Pipeline

Four steps, fully automated after setup.

A custom pipeline connects your stack, runs the analysis, and delivers an interactive report, without you touching anything after onboarding.

01

Connect your stack

Your store platform, ad accounts, and email tool are connected via API during a single onboarding session. No CSV exports, no manual uploads, ever.

The Weekly Cycle

What happens between one Monday and the next.

Once connected, this cycle repeats automatically. The only input required from you is an approve or dismiss click.

SundayOvernight

The pipeline pulls the week's data Automatic

Order data, ad spend, attributed revenue, and return activity from the past 7 days are pulled from Shopify/WooCommerce, Meta, Google, and Klaviyo via API.

SundayLate evening

True ROAS is recalculated per campaign Automatic

Cost of goods, the 30-day return-rate adjustment, and platform fees are blended against attributed revenue to produce a true profitability figure for every active campaign.

MondayBefore 9am

Three decisions are delivered Report sent

Each decision includes a confidence score, an estimated dollar impact, and, where relevant, a note on the 30-day return-data lag. Every decision has an "Approve" and a "Dismiss" action.

Monday onwardOn your action

You approve, dismiss, or do nothing Your decision

Dismissing makes no API call. Approving executes the proposed change, capped at 30% of the current weekly budget, with a 24-hour rollback window.

Within 24hIf approved

The change can still be reversed Rollback window

Any executed action can be undone within 24 hours directly from the report, without needing to log into the ad platform.
