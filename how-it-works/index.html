From connected accounts to a decision, every Monday.

Setup takes a few minutes. After that the same five steps repeat every week, and your only job is to approve or dismiss.

  1. Connect your store and ad accounts

    Once, in a few minutes

    Link Shopify and Meta through each platform's own login. Product costs come from Shopify, or you enter an average cost-of-goods percentage.

  2. True ROAS is calculated

    Automatically, every week

    Cost of goods, returns and fees are subtracted from reported revenue, per campaign. Returns are counted on a 30-day lag, so a campaign is not judged before its refunds arrive.

  3. Three decisions arrive on Monday

    Before 9am

    Each decision names the campaign, the move, the expected dollar impact and how confident the calculation is.

  4. You approve or dismiss

    Your call

    Dismiss a decision and nothing changes. Approved changes are capped at 30% of the current budget. Pushing approved changes to Meta is coming soon, and until then you apply them yourself.

  5. Reverse it if you need to

    Within 24 hours, once execution launches

    Any change pushed from a report can be rolled back from the same report.
