From ad data to real profitability in five steps.

Setup takes a few minutes. After that the same steps repeat every week, and your only job is to approve or dismiss.

Connect accounts

Once, in a few minutes

Link Shopify and Meta through each platform's own login.

We collect your data

Automatic

Orders, returns and ad spend are pulled in. Product costs come from Shopify, or you enter a percentage.

We calculate true ROAS

Every week

Returns, cost of goods and fees are subtracted per campaign. Returns are counted on a 30-day lag.

Three decisions arrive

Monday before 9am

Each names the campaign, the move, the expected impact and how confident the calculation is.

You approve or dismiss

Your call

Dismiss and nothing changes. Changes are capped at 30% of the budget.

Pushing approved changes to Meta is coming soon. Until then you apply them yourself, and rollback within 24 hours applies once execution launches.
