Questions

Pricing and approvals, in detail.

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?

Your account moves to the Free plan and keeps your data. You receive a written summary of the decisions delivered and their estimated impact. You are only charged if you choose a paid plan.

What does the Free plan include?

One store, one ad platform, weekly true ROAS and your top decision each Monday. The other two decisions and the full history unlock on a paid plan.

How is my tier decided?

By your monthly ad spend across connected platforms, reviewed at the start of each billing cycle. If your spend changes significantly, the tier is adjusted with notice before any change takes effect.

How does annual billing work?

Annual billing takes 20% off the monthly price of your tier and is billed once a year. You can switch back to monthly at renewal.

How much can one decision change my budget?

A maximum of 30% per decision, per week. The limit is enforced in the execution logic itself, not only stated as policy, and any executed change can be rolled back within 24 hours.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You receive a CSV export of your data and a JSON export of your pipeline logic. Nothing is switched off without your knowledge.

Is any of this automatic?

The analysis and reporting run automatically. Execution does not. Every approved decision needs an explicit click, and dismissing a decision makes no API call and changes nothing.
