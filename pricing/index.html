Questions

Pricing and execution, in detail.

What happens if the 30-day trial doesn't show enough value?+
You receive a written summary of decisions delivered and their estimated impact either way. If it isn't compelling, you walk away with a full data export and no further obligation.
How are the retainer tiers determined?+
By your monthly ad spend across connected platforms, reviewed at the start of each billing cycle. If your spend changes significantly, the tier is adjusted accordingly, with notice before any change takes effect.
How much can a single decision change my ad budget?+
A maximum of 30% per decision, per week. This limit is enforced in the execution logic itself, not just stated as policy. Any executed change can also be rolled back within 24 hours.
What happens to my data and pipeline if I cancel?+
You receive a full JSON export of your pipeline logic and a CSV export of your data. To keep the pipeline running automatically without the weekly analysis layer, switch to the $29/mo Maintenance Plan. Nothing is switched off without your knowledge.
Is any part of this automatic?+
The analysis and reporting run automatically. Execution does not. Every approved decision requires an explicit click, and dismissing a decision makes no API call and changes nothing.
