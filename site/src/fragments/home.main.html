Questions

What people ask first.

What is true ROAS?

Reported revenue after returns, cost of goods and payment fees, divided by ad spend. It shows what each ad dollar leaves you with, not what it brought through the door.

What does 7Captur connect to?

Shopify and Meta Ads today. Google Ads and WooCommerce are next. If you cannot connect an account yet, you can upload an export instead.

Will it change my ad account without asking?

No. Every decision needs your approval, and a dismissed decision changes nothing.

Do I have to enter my costs?

Product costs sync from Shopify when they are set there. If not, enter an average cost-of-goods percentage and refine it later.

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?

Your account moves to the Free plan and keeps your data. You are only charged if you pick a paid plan.

Is my data safe?

We request only the access the product needs, and you can disconnect at any time. The details are on the security page.
