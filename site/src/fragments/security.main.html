Data lifecycle

Kept only while your account is active.

In transit

All API connections use TLS. Credentials are held in a managed secrets service, never in plain text or in application code.

At rest

Store and ad account data pulled for analysis is encrypted at rest. Only the systems that build your weekly report can read it.

While your account is active

Store data is kept only while your account is active. Contact form messages are kept for up to 12 months.

When you cancel

On request, all store data is deleted within 30 days of termination. Before deletion you receive an export of your pipeline configuration and report history.

Ownership

You own your logic and your data.

If you cancel at any point, including during the trial, you receive a JSON export of your pipeline configuration and a CSV export of your report history.
